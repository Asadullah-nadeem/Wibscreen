Overview

Wibscreen is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your personal data. We take privacy seriously — and we keep it simple: we collect as little data as possible to provide a functional and personalized experience.

By using Wibscreen, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. We ensure that your data is handled with the highest level of care and security.

Data We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to make the platform work for you:

  • Account data: Email address and name provided at sign-up. This is used solely for authentication and communication regarding your account.
  • Workspace data: Collections and website URLs you save — stored locally in your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly sync it (if such features are enabled).
  • Preference data: Theme, font, layout settings — stored locally only to ensure your workspace looks exactly how you want it every time you return.
  • Usage Logs: Minimal technical data such as IP addresses and browser types may be logged for security and diagnostic purposes only.
  • We do NOT collect: Browsing history, website content, analytics, or any behavioral data. We don't track what you do inside the websites you visit.
How We Use It
  • Email is used only for account identification, password resets, and essential service updates.
  • Workspace data is stored locally on your device — we never transmit, read, or analyze your saved URLs.
  • No data is sold, shared, or monetized for advertising purposes.
  • No advertising networks or tracking pixels are used on our platform.
  • We may use aggregated, non-identifiable data to improve our service's technical performance.
Data Storage and Sharing

All workspace data (collections, tabs, settings) is stored exclusively in your browser's localStorage. This means:

  • Data stays on your device only and is not accessible by Wibscreen servers.
  • Clearing your browser data will remove your workspace unless you have backed it up.
  • Third-Party Sharing: We do not share your personal information with third parties, except as required by law or to protect our rights.
  • Service Providers: We may use third-party services for hosting or email delivery, but they are prohibited from using your data for any other purpose.
Your Rights and Choices
  • Access & Update: You can view and update your account information at any time within your profile settings.
  • Delete: You can delete your account and all associated data. Clear your browser's localStorage to remove all local workspace data instantly.
  • Opt-Out: Since we don't use tracking cookies or marketing emails, there's nothing to opt out of except essential service notifications.
  • Data Portability: Your workspace data is yours — it lives in your browser, and we aim to provide tools to export this data in the future.
Security Measures

We use industry-standard security practices to protect your data. Your local data is as secure as your device. We use SSL/TLS encryption for all data transmitted between your browser and our servers. We recommend:

  • Using strong, unique passwords.
  • Keeping your browser and operating system up to date.
  • Being cautious when adding URLs from untrusted sources.

See our Security page for more technical details.

Children's Privacy

Wibscreen does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from anyone under the age of 13. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under age 13, we will delete that information as quickly as possible. If you believe that a child under 13 may have provided us personal information, please contact us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

Contact Us

Questions about privacy or your data? We're here to help. Contact us via the Support page or email us at privacy@wibscreen.com.
