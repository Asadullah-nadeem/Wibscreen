Trust & Safety

Security at Wibscreen

We take your security seriously. Here's exactly how we protect you and your workspace data.

Local Storage Only

All workspace data stays on your device. We never transmit or store your data server-side.

No Tracking

Zero analytics scripts, zero tracking pixels, zero third-party data collection on your usage.

Session Safety

Session tokens stay local. We use browser-native storage mechanisms with no cross-site exposure.

No Ads / Scripts

No ad networks, no foreign scripts, no third-party JavaScript that could compromise your data.

Data Storage and Privacy

Wibscreen employs a "Local-First" data model. Your most sensitive information—collections, URLs, and notes—is stored using browser localStorage. This storage is origin-locked, meaning only Wibscreen can access its own data. This architecture ensures that your private browsing organization never resides on our servers.

Encryption and Transit

All data exchanged between your browser and our servers is protected by industry-standard 256-bit SSL/TLS encryption. This prevents "man-in-the-middle" attacks and ensures that your account credentials and subscription data remain private while in transit.

Account Security

We use secure authentication mechanisms to protect your profile. Passwords are never stored in plain text; instead, we use advanced salted hashing algorithms. We also monitor for suspicious login activity to proactively protect your account from unauthorized access.

Browser Isolation (Iframes)

Every website you load inside Wibscreen runs within its own sandboxed iframe. This utilizes the browser's native Same-Origin Policy (SOP) to ensure that third-party sites cannot "see" each other or access your Wibscreen data. We leverage the web's most fundamental security boundary to keep your workspace safe.

Responsible Disclosure

We value the input of the security community. If you discover a vulnerability, please report it to us through our Support page. We are committed to working with researchers to resolve issues quickly and responsibly.

Safe Usage Tips
  • Use a unique, strong password for your Wibscreen account.
  • Keep your web browser updated to receive the latest security patches.
  • Be mindful of the URLs you add; avoid untrusted or suspicious links.
  • Sign out completely if you are using Wibscreen on a public or shared device.
  • Regularly review your active sessions in your account settings.
