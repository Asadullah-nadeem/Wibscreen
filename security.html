Trust & Safety

Security at Wibscreen

We take your security seriously. Here's exactly how we protect you and your workspace data.

Local Storage Only

All workspace data stays on your device. We never transmit or store your data server-side.

No Tracking

Zero analytics scripts, zero tracking pixels, zero third-party data collection on your usage.

Session Safety

Session tokens stay local. We use browser-native storage mechanisms with no cross-site exposure.

No Ads / Scripts

No ad networks, no foreign scripts, no third-party JavaScript that could compromise your data.

Data Storage Security

Wibscreen stores all collections, tabs, and settings using browser localStorage. This is a sandboxed storage mechanism private to the Wibscreen origin. Other websites cannot access it. Your data does not leave your browser.

Browser Sandbox Security

Websites loaded inside Wibscreen run in sandboxed iframes. They cannot access your Wibscreen data, localStorage, or other open tabs. Standard browser security policies are fully enforced.

Account Security

Currently Wibscreen operates with client-side session flags. We recommend using a strong, unique password on any shared devices. Do not share your device session with untrusted users.

Report a Security Issue

Found a security vulnerability? Please disclose it responsibly via our Support page. We take all reports seriously and respond promptly.

Safe Usage Tips
  • Keep your browser updated to the latest version.
  • Don't add untrusted or suspicious URLs to your workspace.
  • Use a strong password for your device account.
  • Log out when using shared or public devices.
  • Export / back up your workspace regularly.
