About Us

We built the workspace
your browser never had

Wibscreen was created to solve one simple problem — the chaos of too many browser tabs. We believe organized browsing leads to better focus, better work, and better results.

Who We Are

A team that lives in browser tabs

We are a small team of developers and designers who spent years drowning in browser tabs — dozens open at once, hard to find, impossible to organize. We built Wibscreen for ourselves first. Now we are sharing it with everyone.

We believe tools should get out of your way. Wibscreen is fast, clean, and focused on doing one thing extremely well: helping you manage the websites you care about.

Built by developers

Engineering-first team that values simplicity, speed, and clean code above all.

Built for people

Designed with real user workflows in mind — from students to professionals.

Privacy focused

No tracking, no ads, no data collection. Your workspace stays yours.

Free forever

Core features stay free. Always. No credit card, no trials, no limits.

Our Mission

Bring order to the open web

Our mission is to give every person — regardless of technical skill — a smarter, faster, and more organized way to interact with the websites they depend on every day. We want to make web productivity effortless.

Our Vision

The future of personal workspaces

We envision a future where every person has a personalized, structured, and beautiful digital workspace — not just a list of bookmarks or chaotic tabs. Wibscreen is the first step in building that future.

Why We Built It

The problem we had. The tool we built.

The Problem

Too many tabs, no organization, no persistence. Close the browser — lose everything. Reopen — start from scratch. Every. Single. Day.

The Idea

What if your browser could remember your workspace? What if websites lived in organized folders you could switch between instantly without losing your place?

The Solution

Wibscreen. A persistent, organized, beautiful workspace where your websites live in collections and stay saved automatically — forever.
