Overview

Wibscreen is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your personal data. We take privacy seriously — and we keep it simple: we collect as little data as possible.

Data We Collect

We collect only what is necessary:

  • Account data: Email address and name provided at sign-up.
  • Workspace data: Collections and website URLs you save — stored locally in your browser's localStorage.
  • Preference data: Theme, font, layout settings — stored locally only.
  • We do NOT collect: Browsing history, website content, analytics, or any behavioral data.
How We Use It
  • Email is used only for account identification.
  • Workspace data is stored locally on your device — we never transmit or read it.
  • No data is sold, shared, or monetized.
  • No advertising networks or tracking pixels are used.
Data Storage

All workspace data (collections, tabs, settings) is stored exclusively in your browser's localStorage. This means:

  • Data stays on your device only.
  • Clearing your browser data will remove your workspace.
  • No server-side storage of your workspace content.
Your Rights
  • Access: You can view all your data inside the app at any time.
  • Delete: Clear your browser's localStorage to remove all workspace data instantly.
  • Portability: Your data is yours — it lives in your browser, not our servers.
Security

We use standard browser security practices. Your local data is as secure as your device. We recommend keeping your browser and OS up to date for best protection. See our Security page for more details.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Contact us via the Support page.
