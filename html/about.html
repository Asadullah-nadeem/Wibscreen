About Us

We built the workspace
your browser never had

Wibscreen was created to solve one simple problem — the chaos of too many browser tabs. We believe organized browsing leads to better focus, better work, and better results.

The Problem

The browser is broken for deep work

Modern web work is chaotic. We open dozens of tabs for research, communication, and development, only to lose them in a sea of tiny icons. Bookmarks are where links go to die, and browser history is too messy to be a reliable archive. We spend more time managing our tabs than actually doing our work.

We built Wibscreen because we were tired of losing our place every time we closed our browser. We needed a tool that respects our focus and organizes our tools by context, not by chronologically opened tabs.

The Solution

A dedicated workspace for the web

Wibscreen is a productivity layer that sits on top of the web. It transforms the way you interact with websites by organizing them into persistent collections. Instead of searching through tabs, you switch between workspaces designed for specific tasks.

With features like in-app browsing, smart folders, and a "privacy-first" local-only data model, Wibscreen gives you a unified dashboard for everything you do online. It's the browser workspace that keeps you in the flow.

Our Mission

Bring order to the open web

Our mission is to empower every web user with a smarter, more organized way to work. We believe that by reducing the cognitive load of tab management, we can unlock greater creativity and productivity for everyone, from students to seasoned professionals.

Our Vision

The future of digital focus

We envision a future where the web feels like a personalized tool rather than a chaotic stream. Wibscreen is just the beginning of our journey to build the ultimate digital dashboard—a place where your tools, research, and inspiration live in perfect harmony.
