FAQ

Common questions

Can I upgrade from Free to Pro anytime?

Yes. You can upgrade at any time and your workspace data and collections carry over automatically.

What happens if I exceed my login hours on Free?

You'll be prompted to upgrade or your session will reset at the monthly cycle. Your saved data stays intact.

Are the ads intrusive on the Free plan?

Ads on the Free plan are minimal and non-intrusive — displayed in a dedicated area, not overlapping your workspace content.

What does "Bot support" mean?

Bot support is our AI-powered help assistant that can answer questions about using Wibscreen, troubleshoot issues, and guide you through features.

How do I contact Business sales?

Use our Support page and select "Business Inquiry". Our team responds within 24 hours.
