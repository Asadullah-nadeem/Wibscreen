Cookies Policy

Last updated: January 2024. We keep our cookie use minimal and transparent.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by a website. They help websites remember your preferences and session state. Wibscreen uses browser localStorage (not traditional cookies) to store your workspace data — this means no data is sent to servers automatically.

What Wibscreen Stores

We use browser localStorage (similar to cookies but more private) for:

Session Data

Login state flag to keep you signed in between visits.

Workspace Data

Your collections and saved website URLs.

Preferences

Theme (Day/Night), colors, font size, layout settings.

No Analytics Cookies

We do not use Google Analytics, tracking cookies, or advertising cookies.

Third-Party Cookies

Websites you load inside the Wibscreen workspace may set their own cookies. These are governed by those websites' own privacy and cookie policies. Wibscreen has no control over third-party cookies from websites you visit.

How to Control Storage
  • Open your browser settings and clear site data for Wibscreen to remove all stored data.
  • Use private/incognito mode to prevent any localStorage from persisting.
  • Most browsers also allow you to block cookies and localStorage per-site.
Questions?

If you have any questions about how we use storage, contact us on the Support page.
