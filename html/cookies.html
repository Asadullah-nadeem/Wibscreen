Cookies Policy

Last updated: January 2024. We keep our cookie use minimal and transparent.

What Are Cookies and Local Storage?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by a website. Local Storage is a modern browser technology that allows websites to store larger amounts of data locally. Wibscreen uses both to provide a high-performance, personalized workspace experience without needing to store your workspace data on our servers.

How We Use These Technologies

We use browser storage (primarily localStorage) for the following critical functions:

Authentication

Secure session tokens to keep you logged in and verify your identity.

Workspace Data

Your saved collections, URLs, and tab structures are stored on your device.

Preferences

Settings like Dark Mode, font size, and custom layout arrangements.

No Tracking

We do NOT use Google Analytics, tracking pixels, or marketing cookies.

Third-Party Cookies

When you open a website within the Wibscreen workspace, that website may set its own cookies or use its own local storage. These are completely independent of Wibscreen and are governed by those websites' respective policies. We recommend reviewing the privacy settings of any third-party service you choose to use.

Managing Your Storage

You have full control over the data stored in your browser:

  • Browser Settings: You can clear all cookies and site data for Wibscreen via your browser's settings menu at any time.
  • Private Browsing: Using "Incognito" or "Private" mode will ensure that all storage is cleared as soon as you close your browser window.
  • Per-Site Controls: Most modern browsers allow you to block storage for specific sites while allowing it for others.

Note: Disabling storage for Wibscreen will prevent the platform from saving your workspace and keeping you logged in.

Questions?

If you have questions about our use of storage technologies, please visit our Support page or contact us via email.
